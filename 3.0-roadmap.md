# Stride 3.0 — Roadmap (draft)

## Thesis
**From "a tracker I log into" (2.0) → "a coach I check in with every morning" (3.0).**
Drive engagement through *personal, adaptive, kind* daily guidance — not social features
(Stride is single-user by design, so the biggest industry love-lever — social/kudos/leaderboards —
is intentionally off the table).

## What the research said (engaged & loved ≠ most downloaded)
The non-social mechanics that create daily love, ranked:
1. **A daily score / one reason to open** (Whoop recovery, Oura readiness, Apple rings) — the #1 daily-engagement driver.
2. **Adaptive coaching from *your* data** (MacroFactor reverse-calculates TDEE from trend weight + intake).
3. **Kind streaks** (Gentler Streak — moving "Activity Path", rest days count, anti-overtraining, no guilt).
4. Social recognition — *excluded by design.*
5. Frictionless logging · 6. Vivid progress · 7. Transparency/science (show the *why*).

## Decisions locked
- **Readiness = Path A (daytime-only wear).** No bed-wear. Use **resting-HR trend + daytime HRV trend +
  recent training load** (+ optional 1-tap "how do you feel"). Designed for **progressive enhancement** —
  if bed-wear/sleep is ever added, the score sharpens automatically, no rebuild.
- **No food logging** (calorie tracking was explicitly dropped). So Pillar 3 = trend-weight + pace coaching,
  *not* MacroFactor-style intake coaching.
- **No social.**

## The three pillars

### Pillar 1 — "This Morning" (Daily Readiness)  ← LEAD
The reason to open Stride every day.
- A 0–100 readiness signal + one-line guidance: *push / steady / ease up*.
- Inputs (Path A): resting-HR vs personal baseline (heaviest), daytime HRV trend, recent training load;
  optional subjective tap.
- Surfaces in the Today hero. Colour-coded. Shows the *why* ("RHR 4 bpm above your norm").
- **Needs:** enable "Health Metrics" in Health Auto Export (HRV, resting HR); extend `health-ingest` to
  ingest daily metrics → new `health_metrics` table; rolling-baseline maths; Today UI.

### Pillar 2 — "Kind Consistency" (Load-aware streaks)  ← pairs with 1
Sustainable consistency without burnout (the thing that ends weight-loss runs).
- A training-load band vs your own baseline: are you over- or under-doing it this week?
- **Rest days count toward your streak.** Daily suggestion ("4 hard days — recover today").
- Uses synced workouts + readiness. Kind, non-punitive tone.

### Pillar 3 — "Trend & Pace" (Adaptive progress coach)
- Replace the jagged weight line with a smoothed **trend weight** (de-noised, MacroFactor-style).
- Pace coaching: "Trending −0.6 kg/wk; to hit 99 by [date] you're slightly behind" — using real burn
  (Health calories) as context. Honest "at this pace" framing so a bad week ≠ failure.

### Quality bar (cross-cutting, every pillar)
Frictionless logging · vivid progress · **show the why** · kind tone.

## Sequencing
- **3.1 — Readiness (Pillar 1).** Biggest love-per-effort; data pipe already exists. Foundation:
  ingest daily Health metrics + baselines.
- **3.2 — Kind Consistency (Pillar 2).** Builds on readiness + load.
- **3.3 — Trend & Pace (Pillar 3).** Trend-weight + pace coach.
